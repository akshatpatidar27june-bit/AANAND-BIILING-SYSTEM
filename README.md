# Aanand Family Resto — POS & Management System

Centralized multi-outlet restaurant POS and management platform.

## Development roadmap

1. Foundation — organization, outlets, authentication, roles and permissions
2. Outlet setup — counters, tables, kitchen stations and printers
3. Menu — categories, items, variants, outlet pricing and availability
4. POS — dine-in, takeaway, delivery, orders and KOT
5. Billing — GST, discounts, payments, split bills and printing
6. Inventory — ingredients, recipes, purchases, suppliers and transfers
7. Reports — sales, expenses, inventory, outlet comparison and estimated P&L
8. Integrations — payments, WhatsApp, online ordering and printers
9. Production hardening — security, auditability, backups, offline strategy and testing

The architecture is intentionally configurable and does not hard-code the number of outlets, counters, menu items, tables, users or printers.
